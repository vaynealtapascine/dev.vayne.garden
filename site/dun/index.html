Less remembering.
More getting it done.

  • A nudge you can act on

    Mark a reminder done or put it off for a few minutes, right from the notification.

  • Once, or again and again

    Set a one-off reminder, a repeating routine, or a countdown for something happening soon.

  • Still there after a restart

    Your reminders and timers survive reboots. Dun catches up when your device wakes.

  • Your reminders stay with you

    They live on your device. Optional PC-and-phone sync connects directly over your own network, without a cloud account.

Start with your first reminder

Read the guide to reminders, timers, and phone pairing →

  1. Download the Windows installer or Android APK below and open the downloaded file.
  2. Install Dun and open it. On Windows, it can keep running in the small icon area near your clock.
  3. Type a reminder, check the date or duration shown, and add it. When it rings, choose Done or a snooze time.

To share reminders, pair your PC and phone on the same network, or use Tailscale when you’re away. Android alerts depend on notification and exact-alarm permissions, battery settings, and alarm volume.

Moving from an Android development build?

Release APKs since v1.0.0 use a dedicated release signing key, so v1.2.0 installs directly over v1.0.0 and v1.1.0. If you have a debug build, export a backup before uninstalling it, then install this release and restore your backup. Android cannot install it directly over the debug build.
