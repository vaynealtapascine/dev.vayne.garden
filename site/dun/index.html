Dun

Reminders that keep coming back.
Until you’re done.

A reminder is easy to miss. Dun gives you another nudge, and another, until you mark it done. Set reminders and countdown timers on your Windows PC. No account needed.

64-bit PC · v0.3.0 · 28 Sept 2026 · 4.8 MB · .exe installer

Free and open source. Still in development, and usable today.

Try “Laundry in 45 minutes.”

Type it into Dun and check the time it shows before adding it. When the timer ends, Dun reminds you. Busy? Snooze it. Finished? Tap Done and the nudges stop.

You can also try “Pay rent tomorrow at 9” or “Stand up every 45 minutes.”

Less remembering. More getting it done.

  • A nudge you can act onMark a reminder done or put it off for a few minutes, right from the notification.
  • Once, or again and againSet a one-off reminder, a repeating routine, or a countdown for something happening soon.
  • Still there after a restartYour reminders and timers survive reboots. Dun catches up when your device wakes.
  • Your reminders stay with youThey live on your device. Optional PC-and-phone sync connects directly over your own network, without a cloud account.

Start with your first reminder

  1. Download the Windows installer above and open the downloaded file.
  2. Install Dun, then open it from your Start menu. It can keep running in the small icon area near your clock.
  3. Type a reminder, check the date or duration shown, and add it. When it rings, choose Done or a snooze time.
