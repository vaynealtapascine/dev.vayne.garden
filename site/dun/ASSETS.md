# Dun landing-page assets

- timers.webp: recaptured on 2026-10-04 for v1.2.0 (the centered desktop column is capped at 720px, and an unanswered timer turns the shared background deep red) from Dun's local Svelte browser preview at 127.0.0.1:1420, using synthetic demonstration items (Eggs, Bread proof, Pomodoro). Captured at 960×970, including the centered column and surrounding urgency canvas; encoded as WebP at quality 90. No personal reminder data is included.
- icon.svg: unmodified Dun app icon, copied from Dun/assets/app-icon.svg.
- fonts/: unmodified Source Sans 3 fonts shared with the app. See fonts/ORIGIN.md and fonts/SourceSans3-OFL.md for upstream provenance and license.

The page inherits Dun's app palette, humanist type, six-pixel button corners, teal countdown rings, red alerts, and system light/dark appearance. Its layout and explanatory content are adapted for downloading the app on desktop and mobile browsers. Global garden-site pages retain their existing styles.
