SoundOff

A local transcription editor for Windows. Import or record audio and video, transcribe it on your computer, and review word-synced text and speakers. No account, no upload, no hosted inference.

Download for Windows What you need

What it does

  • Import or recordBring in audio or video, or record a microphone, your computer's sound, or both at once.
  • Transcribe on your computerWhisperX runs in a private Python runtime: voice detection, recognition, then word-level alignment.
  • Review in syncPlayback lights up the word being spoken; click any word to jump there. Video plays along.
  • See who spoke whenThe waveform shows each paragraph as a band in its speaker's colour, with a time ruler. Double-click it to play from there.
  • Correct everythingText, speakers, paragraphs and timing are yours to fix, and every save is a revision you can restore.
  • Two viewsRead it as a document, or switch to a subtitle-style table of timed cues.
  • Keep your hands on the keysPlay, skip, step between paragraphs, mark a cue's start and end at the playhead, split, find and replace.
  • ExportText with or without timecodes, SRT subtitles, the clipboard, or a portable project bundle.

What you need

SoundOff is an early preview (0.2.0). Windows is the only platform that has actually been run. The macOS and Linux downloads are built from the same source and can import, edit and export, but they can't play or record.

You'll also need ffmpeg and ffprobe on your PATH, and a one-time private Python runtime for transcription, set up with python scripts/setup_runtime.py. Model packs download only when you ask. The README walks through it.
