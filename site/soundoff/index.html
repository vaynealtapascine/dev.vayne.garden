SoundOff

A local transcription editor. Record or import audio and video, transcribe it on your computer, and review word-synced text and speakers at whatever speed suits you. No account, no upload, no hosted inference.

Download 1.1 for Windows Other platforms

What it does

  • Import or recordBring in audio or video, or record a microphone, your computer's sound, or on Windows both at once.
  • Transcribe on your computerWhisperX runs in a private Python runtime: voice detection, recognition, then word-level alignment.
  • Review in syncPlayback lights up the word being spoken; click any word to jump there. Video plays along.
  • Listen at your paceHalf speed to double, with the pitch kept, so a voice stays itself. Two keys step it up and down.
  • See who spoke whenThe waveform shows each paragraph as a band in its speaker's colour, with a time ruler. Double-click it to play from there.
  • Correct everythingText, speakers, paragraphs and timing are yours to fix. It saves as you pause, and every save is a revision you can restore.
  • Two viewsRead it as a document, or switch to a subtitle-style table of timed cues.
  • ExportText with or without timecodes, SRT subtitles, the clipboard, or a portable project bundle.

New in 1.1

  • ffmpeg includedThe Windows installer brings its own. The Debian package installs it for you.

Downloads and what you need

Windows is the platform SoundOff has actually been run on, end to end on real hardware. The macOS and Linux downloads are the same app, but nobody has opened them on a Mac or a Linux machine yet: treat them as something to try.

Transcription needs a one-time setup that downloads the speech engine, about 2 GB. The Windows installer offers it on its last page; otherwise press Set up transcription in the app. The speech models are another 2 GB, downloaded when you ask. None of the installers are signed, so Windows and macOS will warn you the first time. On a Mac, install ffmpeg with brew install ffmpeg. The README walks through it.
