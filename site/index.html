Tools Before these GitHub

Little tools, grown slowly

We make small things for ourselves and share them. They run on your own device, need no account, and don't send your work anywhere. Three of them run right here in the browser.

Tools

dScribe

  • Runs in your browser
  • Alt text

An alt text editor that describes images and reads their text on your own device. Review, edit, copy, and group images for a post.

Open dScribeSource

eo3

  • Runs in your browser
  • AO3 work skins

Editor of our own: a graph-based HTML generator for fancy AO3 fics, with a live preview of the work page. Built on cpsdqs's prechoster.

Open eo3About & examplesSource

Disclosure Studio

  • Runs in your browser
  • README cards

An editor for AI transparency disclosure cards: pick how much of your code AI wrote and which models wrote it, then export a PNG for your README.

Open Disclosure StudioSource

DryCut

  • Windows
  • macOS
  • Linux

Private, local background removal. Drop, paste or queue images and get transparent PNGs back, all offline.

About DryCutSource

SoundOff

  • Windows
  • Transcription

A local transcription editor. Import or record audio and video, transcribe it on your computer with WhisperX, then review word-synced text and speakers.

About SoundOffSource

Arbor

  • Self-hosted
  • Phone app, works offline

An outliner for tracking projects: nested items, your own statuses and tags, notes, saved views and templates, on your own server.

About ArborSource

Dun

Reminders and countdown timers that keep nudging until you mark them done. Runs on your Windows PC, with an Android app in development.

Meet Dun & download
